🔒 Privacy Policy

Physio Accounting Assistant

Last updated: 5 September 2026

📋 Overview

Physio Accounting Assistant ("the App", "we", "us") is a billing, scheduling and patient-record management tool built for physiotherapy clinics, rehab centers, and similar practices. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and how it is protected. By using the App, you agree to the terms of this policy.

📥 Information We Collect

Account information (clinic owner / practitioner):

  • Name, clinic/business name, designation, and registration details entered at sign-up
  • Email address and mobile number, used for login and account communication

Patient/client records entered by the clinic:

  • Patient name, mobile number, diagnosis/service type, and appointment schedule
  • Billing amounts, payment history, and outstanding balances the clinic records for its patients

Payment information:

  • Subscription payments for using the App are processed by Razorpay, a third-party payment gateway. We do not collect or store your card, UPI, or bank account details — Razorpay handles this directly and shares only the transaction status and ID with us.

Automatically collected data:

  • Device push-notification token (used only to deliver visit/payment reminders)
  • Basic usage/log data (e.g. login timestamps) for troubleshooting and security

🎯 How We Use Information

  • To operate core features: patient records, billing ledgers, calendars, and invoices
  • To send appointment and payment-due reminders via push notification or WhatsApp (opened as a pre-filled message that the clinic staff sends manually — we do not send WhatsApp messages automatically on your behalf without your action)
  • To process and confirm subscription payments
  • To operate the optional Refer & Earn program (tracking referral codes and rewards)
  • To maintain and improve the reliability and security of the App

🗄️ Where Your Data Is Stored

App data (accounts, patient records, billing history) is stored using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication), hosted on Google Cloud infrastructure. Data is transmitted using encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Access to clinic data is restricted to the signed-in clinic account; a system administrator may access data only for support or billing purposes.

🤝 Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database, and push notifications (Firebase Privacy Policy)
  • Razorpay — payment processing for subscription renewals (Razorpay Privacy Policy)
  • WhatsApp (wa.me links) — used only to open a pre-filled message in WhatsApp at the clinic staff's own action; we do not have access to WhatsApp message content

🔐 Data Retention & Deletion

Clinic and patient records are retained for as long as the account remains active, so that billing history and patient ledgers stay accessible. A clinic owner may request deletion of their account and associated data at any time by contacting us (see below). Some records may be retained briefly after deletion where required for fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal obligations.

🧒 Children's Privacy

This App is intended for use by clinic staff and business owners, not by children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. Patient records for minors may be entered by a clinic on behalf of their guardians as part of normal medical/billing record-keeping.

🛡️ Your Rights

  • Request a copy of the data associated with your clinic account
  • Request correction of inaccurate account information
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

♻️ Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
